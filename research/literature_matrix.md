# Adaptive latent computation literature matrix

Audit date: 2026-08-22. I treated a paper as overlapping only after checking
the method description, training target, decision point, and execution
semantics. “Hindsight supervision” means that the target uses a later loss,
reward, or correctness signal unavailable to the controller at deployment.

## Adversarial conclusion

The conjunction “recurrent latent computation + token-dependent depth + no
textual CoT + an explicit maximum” is established. Predicting future benefit
from extra computation is also established at query level by Snell et al. and
at output-quality level by Manvi et al. Most importantly, ANIRA evaluates
online token allocation on controlled computational tasks, while Looped SSM
uses realized next-step loss improvement to refine an exit gate for a looped
Mamba model. Therefore the project must not claim the general research idea,
the use of hindsight loss improvements, or Mamba+looped exits as novel.

The defensible question is narrower: whether a *conditional predictor of the
next-token CE reduction* from the current state, fixed Mamba-3 causal context,
and remaining budget can produce actual token skipping and transfer on
generator-controlled sequential dependencies, with explicit comparisons to
ANIRA-style online halting, convergence/drift exits, and the Looped-Mamba
gate. Phase 1 measures the prerequisite loss curves and does not train a halt
controller.
